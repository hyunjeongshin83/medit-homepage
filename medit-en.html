Telemedicine Sub-Regulations Open for Comment — Effective Dec 24, 2026 (planned)

The Medical Service Act enforcement decree (comments until Nov 2) and enforcement rule (pre-announced Oct 19, 2026; comments until Nov 9) are not final. The draft rule would, in principle, limit first-visit telemedicine to medical institutions in the same province or metropolitan area (if a patient registers an actual residence in the system, that area is used; patients with rare diseases, type 1 diabetes, registered disabilities or long-term-care benefits, among others, are exempt), cap first-visit prescriptions at 7 days in principle (up to 30 days once if there is a treatment or prescription history and the clinician judges continued treatment necessary), and in principle prohibit narcotics, obesity drugs and similar. (Sources: Ministry of Health and Welfare press release and Money Today, Oct 19, 2026) According to press reports, medication delivery would be allowed only for five vulnerable groups, in principle only from pharmacies in the patient's own city, county or district, with an exception up to the province or metropolitan level for rare-disease patients and residents of medically underserved areas. This has not been checked against the pre-announcement text, and details may change. (Sources: Daily Pharm, Oct 18, 2026; Baeksesidae, Oct 23, 2026 — Korean-language press reports)

Digital Medical Products Review Rules Amended — Wider Good-Management-System Exemption

Amended July 27, 2026. Clarifies when clinical data must be submitted or may be waived for software-type digital medical devices, extends the good-management-system exemption to products with no assigned class, and widens the real-world use exemption period to a range of 1 to 3 years. Whether it applies must be checked product by product. (Source: Daily Pharm, July 28, 2026 — Korean-language press report; not yet checked against the notice text)

Privacy Policy

1. Purpose

  • Arranging the demo schedule and replying about the pilot
  • Confirming the date you pick on the booking guide page and answering your system and contract questions
  • Notifying the CEO of new requests, sending the confirmation reply, and preventing repeated or false submissions
  • Setup (PnP) page and program: confirming installation progress for pharmacies that received a serial, and limiting wrong serial entries

2. Items collected

  • Items processed with your consent
    • Required: name and email. A pilot request also requires the pharmacy or organization name.
    • Optional: organization (demo request), phone, preferred date and time slot, message. You can apply without them.
    • Optional (booking guide page, separate consent): consultation answers (type of use you are interested in, type of organization, dispensing software you use, when you would like to start, what you want to know, anything you write). You can still pick a time without them.
    • Stored with your request: the date and time you pick, a random string used only in your own guide page address, the time of receipt, and the policy version you agreed to
  • Items processed without consent, and the legal basis
    • Access records (IP address, time, browser information): the hosting and server environments may record them automatically, for security and abuse prevention. Basis: Article 15(1)6 of the Personal Information Protection Act
    • Setup (PnP): serial (stored only as a hash), pharmacy display name, step results (names of detected dispensing programs, number of registered ODBC data sources, operating system type, number of tables and columns, and so on), and, when a serial is entered wrongly, a hash of the connecting IP address (treated as personal information, not as anonymous data). Basis: setup support requested by the pharmacy that received the serial (Article 15(1)4), and limiting wrong entries (Article 15(1)6)
  • Information we do not want: patient or prescription information, sensitive data such as health information, and unique identifiers such as resident registration numbers. Please do not enter them.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Requests, picked dates and consultation answers: deleted automatically from the database 3 months after receipt (a deletion job runs once a day). Booking guide activity records are deleted with them. Deleted without delay when the data subject requests it.
  • Copies of notification and reply emails in the CEO's mailbox and the run records of the AI automation are not covered by the automatic deletion above. They are deleted too when you ask.
  • Backups and server logs are deleted on the processors' own cycles, so they may not disappear at once.
  • Setup (PnP): an automatic deletion job (serial records 90 days after expiry; IP hashes used to limit wrong entries after 2 days) has been prepared but is not yet in effect. Until it is, we delete them without delay when you ask.
  • Procedure and method: records past the retention period are deleted from the database by the automatic job; deletion requests are carried out by the CEO after confirming your identity through the email address you used to apply.
  • There is no information we must keep under other laws.

4. Provision to third parties

  • We do not provide personal information to third parties, except with the data subject's consent or on a lawful request under the law.
